OUR STORY

A little bit about our Owner & the Restaurant

Kaushal Deshmukh, a 26-year-old owner of Shree Krishna Snacks & Juice, stepped into the hospitality business at a young age, driven by a clear ambition to build something of his own. After completing his education, he turned his long-held dream of starting a food venture into reality. With a strong focus on quality, cleanliness, and heartfelt service, he is steadily building a trusted name among customers.

Shree Krishna Snacks & Juice was started with a simple vision - to serve fresh, delicious food with authentic homely flavours. Conveniently located on NH4 near Shirwal, Satara Road, we offer a range of Maharashtrian and South Indian breakfast favourites, snacks, beverages, and fresh juices.

Our neat, uniformed staff, quick service, welcoming atmosphere, and clean, hygienic restrooms make for a comfortable and refreshing stop for families and travellers on the highway.
